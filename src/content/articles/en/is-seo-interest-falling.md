"Interest in SEO is down 30%" has been repeated since June. It comes from a Rankability report. The number is not invented, but it is narrower than the headlines make it sound, and other data points a different way.

## One headline, four different things

| Source | What it measures | Reported change | Main caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rankability, 15 June 2026 | Google Keyword Planner demand for SEO-related terms, rolling 12 months to May 2026 | About 30% lower than a year earlier | The article we read does not list the SEO keywords or state the country |
| Fractl and Search Engine Land | Monthly search volume of keywords tied to 379 brands, April 2026 against a year earlier | 29% decline reported | High-volume terms only, and not about SEO as a topic |
| Graphite with Similarweb | Organic search visits to over 40,000 large US sites, 2025 against 2024 | Down 2.5% | Graphite sells SEO and AEO services, and Similarweb data is modelled |
| SparkToro with Similarweb | Share of US Google searches ending without a click, January to April 2026 | 68.01%, up from 60.45% in 2024 | Panel data with assumptions, and mobile figures leave out the Google app |

## What Rankability measured

Rankability tracked a fixed panel of 3,751 AI and search-related keywords in Google Keyword Planner, from June 2022 to May 2026. It reports that demand for SEO terms peaked around mid-2025 and then fell about 30%, which it calls the first sustained decline in the panel. It also reports that demand for general AI search terms grew about 3.6 times since 2022, and that GEO attracted roughly twice the demand of AEO.

The report is open about its limits. Its PDF says search interest "measures attention and intent, not revenue or adoption directly" and calls the data one tool's view of Google demand. Its article says Keyword Planner reports bucketed values, so some months were lightly smoothed. In the article we read, the SEO keywords are not listed and the country is not stated.

## Why it does not show that SEO is dying

The claim hides three separate things: how often people search for SEO terms, how much organic traffic sites get, and whether businesses still need SEO. The 30% figure speaks only to the first.

There are other possible readings, and we cannot tell which is right. People may have moved to new words such as GEO and AEO, which Rankability tracks separately. Some may now ask an AI assistant "is SEO dead?" instead of typing it into Google, which Keyword Planner would not count. A peak in mid-2025 may also have reflected a burst of attention that was always going to cool. These are our hypotheses, not findings.

The Fractl figure needs the same care. It reported a 29% decline in search volume, but for keywords tied to 379 brands, not for SEO as a topic, and we could not confirm exactly how the 29% is calculated. Do not quote it as a measure of interest in SEO.

## What the traffic and click data add

Graphite and Similarweb found that organic search visits to over 40,000 large US sites fell only 2.5%, comparing 2025 with 2024, with the top ten sites up 1.6%. Individual sites varied widely, from Quora down 45% to Instagram up 21%. Graphite says plainly that it is a growth agency offering SEO and AEO services, and Similarweb estimates traffic from panels and models, so both the incentive and the method deserve a second look.

SparkToro, using Similarweb's clickstream panel, found 68.01% of US Google searches ended without a click in the first four months of 2026. Its author notes the panels differ across years, that mobile figures rely on an assumption about when a search session ends, and that only 0.34% of searches reached AI Mode.

None of these three studies contradicts the others, because they measure different things. For the wider click debate, see [why the click data disagrees](/seo/organic-click-data-conflicting-reports/).

## How to check your own market

1. **Open Google Trends** and compare "SEO" with a Malay term your customers might use, with the country set to Malaysia. Trends shows relative interest, not search volumes.
2. **Use Keyword Planner** for the terms your buyers search, and remember the ranges are wide.
3. **Check Search Console** for your own impressions and clicks over equal periods. Flat impressions with falling clicks suggests searches are being answered on the results page.
4. **Look at your leads,** not your rankings. If enquiries held steady while a keyword report fell, the headline may not matter to you.

Our guide to [tracking AI traffic in GA4](/ai-search/track-ai-search-traffic-ga4/) shows one way to see where visitors come from.

## What we do not know

We do not know the Rankability keyword list, its country mix or how its buckets were smoothed. We read its published article and PDF, not its raw data, and we did not rerun any figure. We found no Malaysia-specific data on interest in SEO. And we cannot say whether the drop reflects less need for SEO or a change in how people look for it. Treat the 30% as a reason to look at your own numbers, not as a verdict.
