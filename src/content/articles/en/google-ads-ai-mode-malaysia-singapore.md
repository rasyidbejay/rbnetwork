Google said on 28 October 2026 that it is scaling ads in AI Mode to ten more countries, and Malaysia and Singapore are both on the list. This page sets out what is confirmed, what is not, and what is worth checking this month.

We read [Google's announcement](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/) through Search Engine Roundtable, and the help page through PPC Land. We did not open either Google page ourselves, and we say so where it matters.

## What Google announced

- **Countries:** Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore. Brazil, Mexico and the UK are planned for the fourth quarter.
- **Labelling:** sponsored placements are marked as sponsored.
- **Multiturn ads**, which can appear on follow-up questions, launch in the US only.
- **Google's click claim:** click-through rates for sponsored and organic product list items in AI Mode are already similar. Search Engine Roundtable questions whether that holds for text links, since product results are visual. Our reading is that the claim covers one format only.

## What the help page adds

PPC Land read Google's help page on 1 November. Its main points:

- **Language:** English is the only language listed.
- **Eligibility:** existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns enter through the normal auction. No new campaign or budget is needed.
- **Controls:** advertisers cannot opt out of AI Overviews or AI Mode separately from Search, and cannot target them alone.
- **Reporting:** impressions, clicks, conversions and cost sit in standard reports. No separate AI Mode segment is described.
- **Matching:** direct query matching applies when a search shows clear commercial intent. Contextual matching applies when intent emerges from the AI-generated answer, for longer or exploratory questions.
- **Around the ads:** Google's own AI explainers can appear near them, and advertisers cannot write, edit, approve or opt out of those. Billing follows the standard Search model, with no extra charge for the placement.
- **By country:** Indonesia gets AI Overviews only, and Pakistan gets text ads only.

## Why now

Google reported earnings on 28 October. Philipp Schindler, its chief business officer, said ads in AI Mode monetise less than ads in traditional search, according to Search Engine Roundtable and 9to5Google. We did not find a figure for the gap. Several outlets link the expansion to that gap. That link is their reading, and the coverage we read does not show Google saying so about this rollout.

## What we do not know

- **Start dates.** Google says it is scaling, and the sources we read give no date per country.
- **Malay-language queries.** The help page lists English only.
- **Local results.** We have not seen these ads in Malaysia, and no source gave Malaysian performance data.

## What to check this month

1. **Advertisers:** open your search terms report and your broad match and AI Max settings. PPC Land reports that AI Max, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max are eligible for both direct and contextual matching, while exact and phrase match qualify only for direct matching. Because AI Mode has no separate line, judge it by conversions, not clicks alone.
2. **Site owners:** search your five most valuable queries in AI Mode from Malaysia, in English and in Malay. Note the date, whether sponsored results appear, and which sites are cited. Our reading is that paid placements now share the answer area with organic citations, so repeat the check monthly.
3. **Both:** these two countries also have ads in ChatGPT. See [our ChatGPT Ads guide](/ai-search/chatgpt-ads-malaysia-singapore/), [what Google's AI answers link to](/ai-search/google-ai-answers-more-links/), and [why click data disagrees](/seo/organic-click-data-conflicting-reports/).
